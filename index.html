About Us

We are a group of farmers based in Parbhani district of Maharashtra engaged in turmeric farming. We follow sustainable farming practices for cultivating the best quality of raw turmeric. Turmeric is then sun dried in a hygienic environment to prepare dried turmeric fingers. We have an automated in-house turmeric powder manufacturing facility which prepares the finest quality of turmeric powder without any human intervention.

Our Products

Turmeric Fingers

Sun-dried, whole turmeric fingers directly from our farms. Rich in natural oils and aroma.

Turmeric Powder

Pure turmeric powder processed in our automated facility. No additives, just quality.
